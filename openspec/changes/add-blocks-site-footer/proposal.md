# Add SiteFooter

## Why

Public templates duplicate a footer row and the kit has no reusable footer with titled columns, social links or a theme control available on phones.

## What Changes

- Add SiteFooter with columns, centered and simple layouts, optional standalone landmark, brand and newsletter slots, legal and social links, and an icon-only theme switcher.
- Add English and Spanish footer messages and retain caller overrides.
- Replace the landing, pricing and contact footer markup without changing their props or simple row spacing.
- Add fixtures, live gallery examples, accessibility and keyboard tests, generated registries and one changeset.

## Impact

- Capability: blocks/site-footer (ADDED). Risk R1.
- Minor blocks and ui releases; patch templates release.
- No newsletter form, brand marks, locale menu, shared PublicFrame refactor, publishing or deployment.
- Revert this ChangeSet or pin the previous package releases to roll back.
